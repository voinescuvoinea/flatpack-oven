# 0002: Concept B, firebrick barrel vault in a bolted steel frame

Date: 2026-11-07. Status: accepted (user, Phase 1 gate).

## Context
Phase 1 compared A (steel-lined dome), B (firebrick barrel vault) and C (cast
segments). See `docs/reports/phase-1.md` and `docs/concepts/estimates.md`.

## Decision
Concept B, with these Phase 2 starting geometry values (accepted by user):
- Internal floor 0.80 m (span) × 0.90 m (depth); fire along the back wall
- Side walls 0.15 m; segmental arch rise 0.25 m, so the crown is ≈ 0.40 m
- Door ≈ 0.52 m wide × 0.24 m high
- Floor: 64 mm Refarom brick on 2 × 25 mm CaSi board. Vault: Refarom brick,
  thickness to be set in Phase 2 (114 mm standard, 64 mm thin variant)
- Dome insulation: Superwool Plus, thickness set in Phase 2

## Why
Local materials with budget margin, grinder-only fabrication, and closest
match to the published validation oven (Falciano et al. 2022).

## Consequences
- Heat-up time and wood use are the known weak points; Phase 2 quantifies them
  and evaluates the thin-vault variant.
- Top-heat evenness of a barrel vs a dome (K-19) is checked with the radiation model.
