# 0004: Phase 2 design choices

Date: 2026-11-07. Status: accepted (user: "go ahead using your recommendations").

- Vault: 64 mm (bricks laid flat), subject to the Phase 3 thrust-line check (K-26)
- Vault insulation: ≥ 125 mm Superwool-equivalent, hybrid (Superwool hot side + rock wool outside)
- Floor insulation: 100 mm CaSi (4 × 25 mm)
- Expansion: sprung tie rods (≥ 8 mm travel), floating floor with 5 mm perimeter gap,
  5 mm fibre-gasket joints between arch and end walls
- R-P2 (90 min heat-up) kept as the target; the model gives a pessimistic upper bound
  (118 min at 9 kg/h); verify at first firing
